# City National Bank

> City National Bank is a national bank chartered in the United States and a wholly owned subsidiary of Royal Bank of Canada (RBC), headquartered in Los Angeles and known historically as "the bank to the stars." It offers personal, business, and private banking, wealth management, treasury management, and capital markets services. City National runs NO first-party public developer portal or documented public API — consumer-permissioned account data is reached only through third-party aggregators (Plaid, MX, Finicity, Akoya).

## API posture

- No first-party public API. Probes on 2026-07-23: developer.cnb.com and developers.cnb.com do not resolve; api.cnb.com returns 404; no OpenAPI/Swagger at any host root; no /.well-known/ discovery documents; no /llms.txt.
- As a covered depository institution, City National falls under the CFPB Section 1033 Personal Financial Data Rights rule, but no institution-specific 1033 or FDX participation posture is publicly documented.
- Parent RBC operates a separate developer portal (developer.rbc.com) under a different Canadian charter that does not expose City National Bank APIs.
